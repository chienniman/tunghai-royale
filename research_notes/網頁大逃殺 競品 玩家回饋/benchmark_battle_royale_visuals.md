# Benchmark battle royale visuals: how players and critics judged graphics, art direction and assets (state as of 12 November 2026)

**How to read these notes.** Every source statement below is a paraphrase, not a quotation, except one marked direct quote in section 1. Do not put the paraphrased wording in quotation marks in the final report. Confidence tags: **[F]** = I fetched and read the page; **[S]** = taken only from a search-result summary because the page was blocked, paywalled or truncated (this affected PC Gamer, GameSpot, GamesRadar, USGamer, Windows Central, Tom's Guide, Creative Bloq, GameSkinny, Nintendo Life, Zhihu, PTT and Bahamut GNN, among others), so wording, dates and detail are less certain and should be re-checked before being stated firmly. ## 1. PUBG: Battlegrounds — visual reception at launch and over time, and what made its maps feel believable versus cheap

### Takeaway
PUBG launched in 2017 as a realistic-looking game that was visibly assembled from marketplace props, with reused buildings and copy-pasted interiors; it was forgiven because the gameplay was new, and the studio then spent years building assets in-house, remastering maps and protecting frame rate. Players turned out to be attached to the specific maps as they first knew them: one remaster was toned down, another was reverted, and a nostalgia version was reissued. As of 2026 the Unreal Engine 5 visual upgrade is delayed and all-time Steam reviews sit at Mixed (66%).

繁中摘要：PUBG 上市時雖走寫實路線，但明顯使用商城素材、建築與室內大量重複；因玩法新穎而被原諒，之後花多年自製素材、重製地圖並優先保幀數。玩家依戀的是「那張地圖原本的樣子」——重製版不是被調淡就是被還原，官方還推出懷舊版。截至 2026 年，UE5 視覺升級延後，Steam 英文評論為「褒貶不一」66%。

### Cited Findings

**Launch era (2017–2018)**
- Game Informer, 20 Dec 2017, 9.5/10: action looks crisp only if the graphics card can cope; sound quality is called essential to the experience (footsteps, positional gunfire); the two maps are huge, full of things to discover and do not go stale; glitches and unoptimised parts remain. — [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/games/playerunknowns_battlegrounds/b/pc/archive/2017/12/20/looting-shooting-and-staying-alive.aspx) [F]
- Gamereactor, 28 Dec 2017, 8/10: maps are heavily detailed but many assets are somewhat over-used; character animation mostly decent but mantling is rigid; clipping and long-distance pop-in persist; maps are big but not always interesting; the verdict pairs beauty with ricketiness. — [Gamereactor review](https://www.gamereactor.eu/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-review/) [F]
- Critical essay, 7 Dec 2017: Erangel's houses reuse the same models, wallpaper, textures and clutter; towns have no shops or workplaces, so it is hard to imagine a community living there; landmarks (church, school, apartment block, container-filled military base) feel borrowed from other shooters. The author nonetheless concludes the unreality suits the game, because fear of hidden opponents and listening for them shapes the experience more than realistic detail. — [Heterotopias, "Uncanny Island"](https://www.heterotopiaszine.com/2017/12/07/uncanny-island-playerunknowns-battlegrounds/) [F]
- A.V. Club, 22 Dec 2017: Erangel descends from the ArmA 2 map Chernarus and was largely fixed by about September 2016; its spaces are tactically interesting but carry no implied meaning (likened to a paintball arena). Miramar is praised because almost every area has new environmental assets (less copy-and-paste), denser cities with realistic layouts radiating from hubs, and unique sites (casino, hacienda, prison) that give the map meaning. — [A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-new-map-shows-the-imp) [F]
- Art director Tae-seok Jang (undated interview, before the desert map shipped, c. 2017): Erangel is an imaginary island that Brendan Greene wanted set somewhere in Eastern Europe; nobody on the team had been there, so they studied terrain and towns on Google Earth plus brief field research in late 2016; the goal was a map that looks full of real-life objects yet abandoned; an 8x8 km map needed tens of thousands of placed elements; foliage was a major challenge and balancing it for fair play was ongoing; the map had to run on both high- and low-end PCs. — [Green Man Gaming interview](https://www.greenmangaming.com/intel-feature/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/interview-pubg-art-director-tae-seok-jang/) [F]
- Brendan Greene reportedly said Erangel often felt like pieces of other places stitched together and that a remake would give it a clearer Russian/Crimean island identity. — [USGamer interview](https://www.usgamer.net/articles/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-interview-brendan-greene) [S]; [MP1st 2017 report](https://mp1st.com/news/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-erangel-map-to-experience-a-sort-of-remake-pubg-new-map) [S]
- "Asset flip" controversy, June 2018: players matched in-game objects to Unreal Marketplace packs. Kotaku (18 Jun 2018) argued the label was absurd for PUBG: an asset flip originally meant a game hastily assembled from pre-bought assets for quick money, but the term had broadened into a general insult. Communications lead Ryan Rigney explained that a small team must rely on store assets to prototype quickly and cheaply and find the fun; Miramar used some prefab assets strategically; most external assets were modified by PUBG artists; maps three and four used fewer still. A lead artist's reasoning, the one direct quote in these notes: "How many times should a telephone booth be modeled?" — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/no-pubg-is-not-an-asset-flip-1826935848) [F]; [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/pubg-clarify-misunderstandings-about-their-use-of-store-bought-assets) [S]
- World art director Dave Curd, Dec 2018: Erangel was built by a small team with asset-store props, which was appropriate for prototyping; props, buildings and optimisation for later maps were handled in-house. — [PCGamesN Vikendi interview](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/vikendi-making-of-interview) [F]
- Team size: about 15 environment artists built the map when environment artist Carlos Sánchez joined in 2017, on a project of roughly 100 people. — [80.lv interview, 18 May 2021](https://80.lv/articles/creating-diverse-buildings-for-pubg/) [F]
- Chinese-language sentiment, 2018: a Gcores user article (17 Feb 2018) judged PUBG's visuals refined for its genre but badly optimised, with polarised player opinion, and contrasted its realistic, unflashy style with Fortnite's American-comic style and good optimisation. Another Gcores user article (28 Aug 2018) reports that many people's first impression of PUBG was of a shoddily made game (the idiom 粗製濫造), a view the author relays without endorsing. — [Gcores 96419](https://www.gcores.com/articles/96419) [F]; [Gcores 101467](https://www.gcores.com/articles/101467) [F]
- Taiwan forum threads (Bahamut PUBG board, various years): complaints centre on optimisation and stutter; individual posters call the picture merely average, say lowest settings look noisy and heavy anti-aliasing blurs distant targets. — [Bahamut thread 14827](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=30630&snA=14827) [S]; [Bahamut thread 10511](https://forum.gamer.com.tw/C.php?bsn=30630&snA=10511) [S]

**Remaster era (2019–2024)**
- Erangel visual update: announced June 2019 as the first major visual overhaul of the original map; live 24 July 2019 with Season 4 (PC update 4.1); changes ranged from foliage and building colours to reworked terrain. — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-pubg-erangel-map-gets-visual-overhaul) [S]; [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pubg-erangel-update-season-4) [S]
- After test-server feedback the team reduced grass density, colour saturation and overall brightness. — [Wccftech, update 4.1](https://wccftech.com/pubg-pc-update-4-1-live-servers/amp/) [S]
- Dave Curd, 19 Jul 2019: aim was to keep Erangel fresh yet familiar without losing its magic; every building, surface and texture was touched; terrain and foliage realism increased; lighting was adjusted for fairer gunfights and better player reads; set dressing was restrained so small props do not distract or snag players; the stated philosophy puts layout ahead of realism (a flat realistic power-plant car park is less fun than a varied layout), fun first and beauty second, and always protects frame rate. He also praised the cohesion of props, buildings and lighting. — [App Trigger interview](https://apptrigger.com/2019/07/19/pubg-update-41-dave-curd-world-art-director-interview) [F]
- Reception of the Erangel update was mostly favourable but some found it too subtle: one write-up said the map badly needed the makeover and felt fresh again, while a returning casual player (Aug 2019) said it made no real difference. — [AltChar](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/pubgs-erangel-makeover-is-like-a-new-map-adPEz5s5V11K/amp) [S]; [God is a Geek](https://godisageek.com/2019/08/are-seasons-a-reason-to-return-to-pubg/) [S]
- Sanhok was remastered in 2020 and reverted in April 2022 (update 17.1; 13 Apr PC, 21 Apr console). Krafton's 2022 dev plan (24 Mar 2022) says some players liked the revamp but most wanted the original back despite several attempts to modernise it. — [PUBG 2022 dev plan](https://pubg.com/en/news/1594) [F]; [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/pubg-patch-17-1-has-restored-sanhok-to-its-original-glory/1100-6502794/) [S]
- Vikendi (Dec 2018): 36 sq km snow map inspired by the Slovenian countryside; the team made reference trips to Slovenia and also used films, TV and library travel books; layouts were grey-boxed with no art first to find the fun; a large pool of ideas was narrowed to roughly 10–15 key areas; art works on the big picture first and small details second; cover objects, haze and falling snow were added because white snow makes players too easy to spot. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/vikendi-making-of-interview) [F]
- How buildings avoid looking repeated (2021 account): designers set building counts, sizes and interior/window rules for fast looting; references came from site visits or Street View; base building meshes are recycled with different materials, roofs and set dressing; recognisable shapes help players loot faster; for Season 9 nearly every building was about 80% modular to meet a roughly three-month schedule; because modularity can make buildings look too similar, each building gets its own dirt mask and about half the building time goes into small add-on pieces (brick corners, stone bases, roof parts), with decals for leaks, rust and grime added last. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/creating-diverse-buildings-for-pubg/) [F]
- Erangel Classic (PC 14–28 May 2024, console 23 May–6 Jun 2024) reissued the early-access version of the map for players nostalgic for it; press coverage was warm. — [Krafton press release](https://press.krafton.com/PUBG-BATTLEGROUNDS-gets-nostalgic-with-new-Erangel-Classic-map-on-14-M) [S]; [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/battle-royale/we-are-going-home-pubgs-original-erangel-map-from-early-access-is-making-a-triumphant-return-to-the-battlegrounds) [S]

**Current state (2025–November 2026)**
- 2025 roadmap: a move to Unreal Engine 5 was announced, with a visual upgrade as the first noticeable change. — [Krafton 2025 roadmap](https://press.krafton.com/KRAFTON-REVEALS-2025-ROADMAP-FOR-PUBG-BATTLEGROUNDS) [S]
- 2026 roadmap (23 Mar 2026): the team said the UE5 move cannot be a simple engine port, the plan was partly revised, and some service plans aimed at the 2027 tenth anniversary were adjusted; destructible terrain was scheduled for Erangel in April 2026 because familiar open spots (wheat fields, ridgelines) made endgames too dependent on zone luck; smoke becomes interactive; the year is framed as going back to basics on client optimisation and server stability. — [PUBG 2026 roadmap](https://pubg.com/en/news/9855) [F]
- 4 May 2026: the head of the PUBG IP franchise group confirmed the UE5 visual upgrade is delayed. — [MP1st interview](https://mp1st.com/interviews/beyond-battle-royale-pubg-studios-redefining-franchise-new-decade) [F]
- 5 Feb 2026: update 40.1 ended the seasonal "Erangel: Subzero" version (introduced in update 39.1 with snowfall, snow cover and a frozen blue zone), restored the classic map, improved indoor building lighting and added team-status icons and blue-zone progress on the minimap. — [Gamemeca](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1771108) [F]; [Dcard PUBG board, update 39.1 summary](https://www.dcard.tw/f/pubg/p/260431368) [S]
- July 2026: console versions get resolution-priority and frame-rate-priority graphics modes. — [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/07/pubg-is-getting-two-new-graphics-modes-for-xbox-full-resolution-and-fps-details-revealed) [S]; [PUBG dev letter](https://pubg.com/news/10385) [S]
- Steam, read 2026-11-12: All Reviews Mixed, 66% positive of 447,802 English reviews (release date shown as 21 Dec 2017). — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/578080/PUBG_BATTLEGROUNDS/) [F]
- A 2025 Bahamut GNN hands-on of the spin-off PUBG: Black Budget noted higher colour saturation and enemies that no longer blend murkily into the background, implying that murkiness was a known weakness of the original. — [Bahamut GNN](https://gnn.gamer.com.tw/detail.php?sn=297369) [S]
- An undated TheGamer piece argues that PUBG's realistic style makes any low-resolution texture stand out more. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-worth-playing/) [S]

### Inferences
- PUBG shows that a small team can ship believable realism from bought props, but only with a novel game underneath; the cost is a lasting "asset flip" stigma and visible repetition that critics noticed immediately, and the cure took a much larger art team several years.
- What made Erangel believable was not fidelity: it was real-world reference (Google Earth, Street View, field trips), a consistent abandoned Eastern European theme, sheer object density, sound, and terrain that produces tension. What made it feel cheap was repetition of buildings and interiors and landmarks with no implied meaning.
- PUBG's own art lead ranks layout above realism and fun and frame rate above beauty, and every visual update was pulled back when it hurt visibility or familiarity. For a competitive shooter, readability and familiarity outrank graphical ambition.
- The modular-building method (few base meshes, varied materials, roofs, unique dirt masks, add-on detail pieces, decals) is directly transferable to a procedurally generated campus where repetition is the main risk.

## 2. Fortnite, Apex Legends, Call of Duty: Warzone, PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, Naraka: Bladepoint — how their look is judged, and how stylised art has aged against realistic art

### Takeaway
Stylised looks (Fortnite, Free Fire, Apex's semi-comic style) are credited with readability, low hardware cost and longevity, and Fortnite drew sustained complaints in 2024 precisely when it drifted toward realism and muted colour. Realistic looks earn praise mainly through lighting and expensive detail (Naraka, the 2025 Verdansk rebuild) and are attacked the moment colour turns washed-out or enemies become hard to see (Warzone's Caldera).

繁中摘要：風格化（Fortnite、Free Fire、Apex 的半漫畫風）在可讀性、低規格與耐看度上被普遍肯定；Fortnite 在 2024 年往寫實、低彩度靠攏時反而被玩家持續抱怨。寫實作品（永劫無間、2025 重建的 Verdansk）主要靠燈光與高成本細節獲得稱讚，一旦色彩灰濛、敵人看不清（Warzone 的 Caldera）就立刻被罵。

### Cited Findings

**Fortnite**
- Origin of the style: Epic's GDC 2018 talk (Peter Ellis) describes the art evolving over several years from drab, monotone environments to bright, colourful set pieces with more personality, and covers practices for building an AAA free-to-play game with a small art team; earlier coverage records the concept shifting from scary and realistic to playful. — [Game Developer, 6 Aug 2018](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/video-developing-the-stylized-art-of-i-fortnite-i-) [F]; [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024936/Developing-the-Art-of-Fortnite) [S]; [Game Informer, Dec 2014](https://gameinformer.com/games/fortnite/b/pc/archive/2014/12/10/fortnite-art-team-shows-the-evolution-from-scary-to-playful.aspx) [S]
- Critic view, 21 Aug 2025: bold colours and chunky shapes let players spot threats, loot and teammates without enemies camouflaged in scenery; exaggerated visuals avoid the dated look realistic games acquire within a few years; the style runs across hardware from high-end PCs to phones; and it absorbs outside characters into one coherent look. Caveat: an opinion feature with no data and a promotional plug. — [PlayStation Universe](https://www.psu.com/news/the-secret-genius-of-fortnites-art-style/) [F]
- Player pushback against realism, 25 Jan 2024 (Chapter 5 Season 1): Reddit users said the game had moved from its vibrant cartoon style to a duller realistic look and asked for the old style and wackier content back; a minority said both styles work. — [CharlieIntel](https://charlieintel.com/fortnite/fortnite-players-beg-devs-to-ditch-boring-look-bring-back-cartoony-art-style-300007) [F]
- 7 Apr 2024 (Chapter 5 Season 2): players objected to Mediterranean-style towns and colosseums replacing the earlier small suburbs and restaurants, calling it a boring hyper-realistic phase; a counter-view preferred a map with very different areas over repeated suburbs. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/fortnite/players-want-fortnite-to-ditch-boring-hyper-realism-and-return-to-its-roots-2633891) [F]
- 30 Jun 2024 (Chapter 5 Season 3): players called the season's colours dull, asked for green grass and vibrant colour in all modes, said they missed the vibrancy of older seasons, and blamed Unreal Engine 5 realism for worse performance; the Reload mode had just reintroduced the older look. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/fortnite/fortnite-players-call-for-visual-changes-over-dull-colors-in-chapter-5-season-3-2802239) [F]
- A YouTube critique (summarised) argues the cartoon look was gradually replaced by a more realistic, generic aesthetic to accommodate collaborations and that many players see Chapter 4 as the visual peak. — [Recapio summary of "Why Fortnite Lost Its Artstyle"](https://recapio.com/digest/why-fortnite-lost-its-artstyle-by-i-eat-h2o) [S]
- Nostalgia for the original look and map: the Fortnite OG season (launched 3 Nov 2023, restoring the 2018 map) produced Epic's biggest day, 44.7 million players and 102 million hours, with about 6.2 million concurrent per a third-party tracker (outlets differ on whether the day was 4 or 5 Nov). — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/fortnite-delivers-biggest-day-in-history-amid-layoffs-at-epic-games) [S]; [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/the-return-of-fortnites-original-map-brings-a-record-setting-6m-players-to-the-game) [S]
- Chapter 7, 1 Dec 2025: players accused Epic of using AI-generated art for in-world posters and decals (a yeti poster with the wrong number of toes was the main exhibit), saying it made the game feel auto-generated and less curated; some said they would quit. No Epic statement was included. — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/fortnites-latest-update-allegedly-uses-ai-art-gamers-say-it-ruins-authenticity-1759318) [F]
- Chinese-language framing, 2018: Fortnite's comic style and exaggerated effects are contrasted with PUBG's realism, and Fortnite is said to stay clean (no jaggies or tearing) even at very low settings. A Taiwan report of Newzoo data (9 Aug 2018) found about 40% of battle royale players played both games, Fortnite's players were younger and more casual, and the writer attributes this partly to a more mass-market art style and lower system requirements (the writer's interpretation, not measured). — [Gcores 96419](https://www.gcores.com/articles/96419) [F]; [TechNews Taiwan](https://technews.tw/2018/08/09/player-reporter-pubg-vs-fortnite/) [F]

**Apex Legends**
- Reviews describe a not-quite-cartoon style that is endearing with impressive visuals (2020), a colourful direction that keeps characters and environments distinguishable with legible silhouettes in fast duels, and a blend of science-fiction realism and comic-book grit. — [The Boar](https://theboar.org/2020/03/apex-legends-pc-review/) [S]; [jeu.video](https://jeu.video/en/article/apex-legends-review) [S]; [Gamer Braves](https://www.gamerbraves.com/?p=31947) [S]
- A 2025 review notes the heavily modified Source engine does not push photorealism but the art direction is strong; an art-book reviewer found the art accomplished but potentially generic among shooters. — [Egg Network](https://egg.network/reviews/apex-legends) [S]; [Parka Blogs](https://parkablogs.com/comment/29906) [S]
- Visual clutter, 9 Jan 2022: players said stacked ability effects and hit/damage cues made it hard to see what they were shooting at; a Respawn producer said he had raised it internally. — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/apex-legends-developer-suggests-plan-to-tackle-visual-clutter-3133025) [F]
- 2026: a forum user reported visual discomfort and aiming trouble after a Season 29 material and lighting update, and a 28 May 2026 patch adjusted lighting on Kings Canyon and Olympus. Single-user evidence only. — [EA forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/apex-legends-feedback-en/visual-discomfort-and-aim-issues-since-s29-update/13793979) [S]; [patched.gg](https://patched.gg/games/apex-legends/apex-legends-latest-update-5282026) [S]

**Call of Duty: Warzone**
- PC Gamer argued Caldera looked as washed-out and bland as Warzone always had, that the look worked for the 2020 Verdansk launch but felt stale by Verdansk '84. — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/what-happened-to-all-the-color-in-call-of-duty-warzones-pacific-map) [S]
- 10 Dec 2021 (Caldera launch): players reported missing textures on buildings, ground and trees, distorted weapon models, and one commenter called the colours awful and the graphics dated. — [CharlieIntel](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-warzone/warzone-players-slam-awful-pacific-caldera-graphics-issues-153450/) [F]
- Visibility was the larger complaint: players said they were dying because they could not see enemies, and praised the Season 3 (2022) fix. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/call-of-duty/warzone-players-still-furious-with-caldera-visibility-ruining-their-matches-1766414) [S]; [CharlieIntel](https://charlieintel.com/warzone-players-delighted-as-devs-finally-fix-caldera-visibility-issues/174364/) [S]
- Verdansk's return, 4 Apr 2025: players praised the rebuilt map's look and especially its lighting, said it played very close to the original and brought back good memories; no other Warzone map was as well received and the newest map (Area 99) was very unpopular; criticism was sparse and about time-to-kill. — [MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/players-rejoice-over-verdansk-in-cod-warzone-as-they-no-longer-have-to-play-the-garbage-of-the-last-3-years,1246236) [F]; [Military Times, 11 Apr 2025](https://www.militarytimes.com/off-duty/military-culture/2025/04/11/return-to-verdansk-the-realism-evolution-of-call-of-duty-warzone/) [S]

**PUBG Mobile and Free Fire**
- PUBG Mobile (2018) was reviewed as a surprisingly good port with graphics and frame-rate presets for weaker phones; the strongest visual praise came from high-end devices and the lowest setting still struggled in busy areas. — [Lowyat](https://www.lowyat.net/2018/158229/pubg-mobile-lightning-review-surprisingly-good-mobile-port/) [S]; [Tom's Guide roundup](https://www.tomsguide.com/us/pubg-mobile-reviews,news-26806.html) [S]; [Gamebrain user summary](https://gamebrain.co/game/pubg-mobile) [S]
- Free Fire analysis, 8 Nov 2019: about 900 MB and playable on 1 GB RAM while PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty Mobile needed at least 2 GB and Fortnite 3 GB; on a 1 GB phone shadows and most light effects vanish and the world looks pixelated, but shooting and movement are barely affected; two small, less detailed maps with fewer locations; 50 players and about 10 minutes per match versus about 20 in PUBG Mobile; no single art theme, with pop-culture-inspired characters and regionally tailored skins; clear feedback such as the reticle turning red on target. The author credits low-end optimisation for its edge in South America and Southeast Asia. — [Game Developer blog](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/analysing-the-success-of-free-fire-the-other-king-of-battle-royal) [F]
- Free Fire's graphics were the most common target of criticism, yet the game passed one billion Google Play downloads by August 2021; Garena's team said it was intentionally aimed at low-performing devices; comparisons call PUBG Mobile cleaner, sharper and more realistic and Free Fire cartoonish, a look said to suit its quirky characters. — [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Free_Fire_MAX) [S]; [GameMakers interview](https://gamemakers.beehiiv.com/p/garena-free-fire-secrets-success-analyzing-fall) [S]; [Sportskeeda comparison](https://sportskeeda.com/free-fire/free-fire-vs-pubg-mobile-which-game-better-graphics-fps-support-recent-updates) [S]
- Garena launched the higher-fidelity Free Fire MAX on 28 Sep 2021; in April 2026 it added an experimental 60 FPS option that reportedly almost nobody enabled. — [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/2021/09/28/garena-free-fire-max-brings-a-graphics-and-gameplay-overhaul-to-the-popular-arena-shooter/?amp) [S]; [Free Fire Mania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/news/60-fps-in-free-fire-experimental-feature-released-and-almost-no-one-activated-it.html) [S]

**Naraka: Bladepoint**
- 2021 reviews consistently praise the visuals: GameSpot calls Morus Island gorgeous (golden valleys, shipwrecks, ruined temples evoking Chinese myth); Prima Games says the lighting makes players want to stop fighting and look; Tom's Guide praises visuals and animation; Wccftech lists visuals as a strength but criticises a cluttered interface; GameSpot adds that most characters read as generic martial artists. — [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/videos/naraka-bladepoint-video-review/2300-6455997/) [S]; [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/naraka-bladepoint-review) [S]; [Tom's Guide](https://tomsguide.com/reviews/naraka-bladepoint) [S]; [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/review/naraka-bladepoint-review-musou-royale/) [S]

### Inferences
- Stylised art has aged better in the sense that players actively ask for the 2018 Fortnite look back, while realistic games must be periodically rebuilt (Verdansk 2025, PUBG remasters, PUBG's UE5 plan) to stay acceptable.
- Fortnite's 2024 backlash was about colour, material realism and architecture, not about rendering technology: the lesson is to keep saturation, exaggeration and playful shapes, not to avoid modern lighting.
- Free Fire is the strongest evidence that performance and reach on weak hardware outweigh visual fidelity, which is directly relevant to a browser game: its graphics were criticised throughout its rise.
- Lighting is the single most praised attribute in realistic titles and washed-out colour the most attacked; both are cheap to control relative to asset fidelity.
- Readability is treated as a visual-quality criterion in every title: Fortnite is praised for it, Apex and Warzone are criticised when they lose it.

## 3. Small-team and low-budget battle royales — which were praised or panned for their look, and why

### Takeaway
Small teams that chose a coherent, deliberately low-fidelity identity (Super Animal Royale's 2D animals, Totally Accurate Battlegrounds' physics comedy, Unturned's blocky world, and the non-battle-royale BattleBit Remastered) hold Very Positive Steam ratings years later; small or rushed teams that shipped realism or an unfinished look (Radical Heights, Fear the Wolves, Islands of Nyne) were called unfinished, derivative or unoptimised, and Islands of Nyne lost its players within months. ## 5. Evidence that a coherent stylised or low-poly look is received better than imperfect realism for small teams, and the counter-examples

### Takeaway
Developers and critics state the case explicitly and consistently: stylised and low-poly art is cheaper and faster to produce, easier for a small team to keep consistent, runs on weaker hardware and dates more slowly. The strongest counter-examples are PUBG and Free Fire, which succeeded with bought-asset realism and widely criticised graphics respectively because gameplay novelty and performance mattered more, and Spellbreak, whose stylised art was praised but which still closed.

繁中摘要：開發者與評論的明確說法一致：風格化／低多邊形成本低、製作快、小團隊較能維持一致、低規格可跑、較不易過時。最強的反例是 PUBG（靠商城寫實素材）與 Free Fire（畫面一直被嫌但低規格跑得動），證明玩法新穎與效能可以壓過美術；另外 Spellbreak 的風格化美術受好評，卻仍然關服。

### Cited Findings

**Explicit statements for stylised or low-poly**
- Astroneer artist Paul Pepera (blog dated 15 Feb, year not shown): with only two full-time artists, the flat-shaded low-poly style lets them make game-ready assets in a day or two, removes texture creation entirely, avoids the high-resolution baking and complex shaders that make AAA assets a time and budget sink, and still makes a bold visual statement; advice to other indies is to pick a direction that fits the team and reduces complexity and risk. — [Astroneer blog](https://blog.astroneer.space/?p=7) [F]
- Epic's GDC 2018 Fortnite art talk is framed as practices for making an AAA free-to-play game with a small art team after moving from drab to bright stylised visuals. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/video-developing-the-stylized-art-of-i-fortnite-i-) [F]
- GDC 2017, Ethan Redd, "Low Poly Modeling: Style Through Economy": the session description presents low-poly 3D as suited to teams with few artists or little time, much as pixel art was for earlier indies, and covers avoiding common mistakes and making smart style choices. — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024652/contactUs) [S]
- GDC 2023, Vincent Perea, "Big Visuals, Small Budget: Get the Most from a Lean Art Team": premise is that every indie game needs a marquee look to stand out and that a small team can achieve it by working smarter. — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1028724/Art-Direction-Summit-Big-Visuals) [S]
- Raphael Colantonio (Arkane co-founder, WolfEye), reported 26 Aug 2024: prefers stylised art because ultra-realism that once looked stunning becomes hard to look at, and stylisation takes less time to produce. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/rpg/dishonored-co-creator) [S]
- Guild Wars 2's art direction is described by its makers as aiming for timelessness, because photorealism ties a game to the technology of its day. — [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/digital-art/we-focused-on-creating-something-timeless-why-guild-wars-2-remains-an-artistically-unique-game) [S]
- Critic view (2025): Fortnite's style is called future-proof where realistic games date within a few years. — [PlayStation Universe](https://www.psu.com/news/the-secret-genius-of-fortnites-art-style/) [F]
- Practitioner view (2026): coherence is read as quality, so a modest style executed consistently beats a patchwork of better assets; recommended constraints are limited palettes, flat shading and silhouette-driven design. — [Bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/art-direction-beats-art-budget) [F]
- Chinese-language commentary holds that cartoon rendering is simpler than realistic rendering and helps explain Fortnite's lower hardware demands. Seen only in a search summary of several Zhihu articles; the exact page was not confirmed, and these are two of the returned candidates. — [Zhihu article](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/30852251) [S]; [Zhihu question](https://www.zhihu.com/question/265380283) [S]

**Outcome evidence**
- Small-team stylised or low-poly games holding Very Positive Steam ratings on 2026-11-12: Super Animal Royale 92%, Totally Accurate Battlegrounds 89%, Unturned 90%. — [Steam SAR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/843380/Super_Animal_Royale/) [F]; [Steam TABG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/823130/Totally_Accurate_Battlegrounds/) [F]; [Steam Unturned](https://store.steampowered.com/app/304930/Unturned/) [F]
- BattleBit Remastered: three developers, low-poly, Steam top seller with over 61,000 concurrent in June 2023. — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/06/20/battlebit-remastered-61k-ccu-top-selling-steam) [F]
- Realism from a rushed or small team panned as unfinished or derivative: Radical Heights and Fear the Wolves. — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/early-access-review-radical-heights/) [F]; [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/?p=61981) [S]
- When Fortnite moved toward realism in 2024, players asked for the cartoon look back. — [CharlieIntel](https://charlieintel.com/fortnite/fortnite-players-beg-devs-to-ditch-boring-look-bring-back-cartoony-art-style-300007) [F]; [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/fortnite/players-want-fortnite-to-ditch-boring-hyper-realism-and-return-to-its-roots-2633891) [F]

**Counter-examples and limits**
- PUBG: realistic, built by a small team from asset-store props, became the genre's defining hit; its developers defended bought assets as the only sensible way for a small team to prototype. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/no-pubg-is-not-an-asset-flip-1826935848) [F]; [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/vikendi-making-of-interview) [F]
- Free Fire: graphics criticised throughout, yet over a billion downloads, attributed to running on 1 GB phones. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/analysing-the-success-of-free-fire-the-other-king-of-battle-royal) [F]; [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Free_Fire_MAX) [S]
- A well-liked look did not ensure survival: Spellbreak (stylised, closed Jan 2023), Islands of Nyne (considered attractive, dead within months of early access), and BattleBit's collapse to a few hundred players by 2026 for lack of updates. — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/wizard-royale-spellbreak-is-shutting-down-next-year) [S]; [MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/islands-of-nyne-tot-was-ist-passiert,309740) [S]; [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/battlebit-remastered) [S]
- High-fidelity realism from well-funded studios is praised: Naraka (2021) and Ring of Elysium (2019). — [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/naraka-bladepoint-review) [S]; [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/ring-of-elysium-review/) [F]
- Low-poly can be dismissed on first sight (BattleBit compared to Roblox), and generic low-poly battle royales on Steam attract almost no reviews. — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/06/20/battlebit-remastered-61k-ccu-top-selling-steam) [F]; [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2790190) [S]
- Jeff Vogel (26 Aug 2019) argues from the other side that visibly cheap art is a viable business choice for a niche audience, that past graphical upgrades did not change critics' minds, and that improving graphics costs a lot with no guaranteed sales return; he also notes a generic style lets freelancers be replaced without breaking consistency. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/why-all-of-our-games-look-like-crap) [F]
- Some players simply dislike cartoon looks: one Darwin Project critic objected to its Fortnite-like graphics. — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/8834/darwin-project/reviews?page=1) [S]

### Inferences
- The balance of evidence supports a coherent stylised or low-poly direction for a team that cannot reach big-studio realism: every explicit developer statement found points that way, and the small-team games with lasting good reviews all took it.
- The counter-examples do not show that imperfect realism works on its own merits; they show that a novel game or exceptional reach on weak hardware can override art. A browser game's equivalent of Free Fire's advantage is instant access and smooth frame rate, which stylised art also protects.
- Realism raises three costs a small team cannot avoid: texture and material production, exposure of every low-resolution surface, and continual rework as standards move. Flat or low-poly art removes the first two and slows the third.
- The style has to be distinctive and tied to the setting and tone; simple but generic is not rewarded.
- Art direction will not secure retention; it buys a fair first impression and review tone, after which content, performance and community decide.

### Gaps
- No post-mortem was found in which a small battle royale team directly compares attempting realism with attempting stylisation on the same project.
- The GDC talks were seen only as session descriptions; the full arguments were not reviewed.
- The Colantonio and Guild Wars 2 statements rest on search summaries and headlines because the articles could not be opened.
- No quantitative study links art style to review score or retention for small-team shooters.
- Possible further leads not opened: Shedworks on Sable's readability (Game Developer) and Jane Ng on Firewatch's copy-pasted wilderness (PCGamesN).

## 6. How important is the map itself — recognisable landmarks, real-place inspiration, points of interest

### Takeaway
The map is central to how players judge and remember a battle royale: the genre's biggest nostalgia events (Fortnite OG, Verdansk's return, Erangel Classic, the Sanhok reversal) were all about getting a specific, familiar map back. Developers ground maps in real places through cheap reference methods, but put layout ahead of realism and give each area a distinct, nameable landmark; critics separate maps with meaningful, unique places from ones that feel like repeated arenas.

繁中摘要：地圖是玩家評價與記憶大逃殺的核心：Fortnite OG、Verdansk 回歸、Erangel Classic、Sanhok 還原等最大的懷舊事件，全都是「把熟悉的那張圖要回來」。開發者用低成本方式（Google Earth、街景、實地考察）取材於真實地點，但堅持「佈局優先於寫實」，並讓每個區域有可喊點的獨特地標；評論會區分「有意義、獨特的地點」與「重複的競技場」。

### Cited Findings

**Player attachment to specific maps**
- Fortnite OG (Nov 2023), restoring the 2018 map, gave Fortnite its biggest day at 44.7 million players. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/fortnite-delivers-biggest-day-in-history-amid-layoffs-at-epic-games) [S]
- Verdansk's return (Apr 2025) was the best-received Warzone map event, with players stressing how close it felt to the original. — [MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/players-rejoice-over-verdansk-in-cod-warzone-as-they-no-longer-have-to-play-the-garbage-of-the-last-3-years,1246236) [F]
- PUBG reverted Sanhok in 2022 because most players wanted the original, and reissued early-access Erangel in 2024 for nostalgic players. — [PUBG 2022 dev plan](https://pubg.com/en/news/1594) [F]; [Krafton press release](https://press.krafton.com/PUBG-BATTLEGROUNDS-gets-nostalgic-with-new-Erangel-Classic-map-on-14-M) [S]
- Fortnite players in April 2024 asked for the classic suburbs back, while others wanted a map made of very different areas. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/fortnite/players-want-fortnite-to-ditch-boring-hyper-realism-and-return-to-its-roots-2633891) [F]
- Game Informer's 9.5 launch review of PUBG (Dec 2017) singled out the maps as huge, full of things to discover and not growing stale over hundreds of hours; Gamereactor's 8/10 review called them big but not always interesting. — [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/games/playerunknowns_battlegrounds/b/pc/archive/2017/12/20/looting-shooting-and-staying-alive.aspx) [F]; [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-review/) [F]

**Real-place inspiration and how it is done**
- Erangel: Eastern European setting researched through Google Earth and brief field work by a team that had not visited the region. — [Green Man Gaming](https://www.greenmangaming.com/intel-feature/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/interview-pubg-art-director-tae-seok-jang/) [F]
- Vikendi: Slovenian countryside, with reference trips plus films, television and travel books. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/vikendi-making-of-interview) [F]
- PUBG building references come from site visits or Street View. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/creating-diverse-buildings-for-pubg/) [F]
- Warzone's Vondel is a fictional Dutch city blending canal systems resembling Haarlem and Utrecht. — [Digital Trends, 3 Aug 2023](https://www.digitaltrends.com/?p=3397705) [F]
- Erangel's art lead says the team put layout ahead of realism: a faithful flat car park is less fun than a varied invented layout. — [App Trigger](https://apptrigger.com/2019/07/19/pubg-update-41-dave-curd-world-art-director-interview) [F]

**Landmarks, points of interest and wayfinding**
- Critics credit Miramar's unique sites (casino, hacienda, prison) with giving the map meaning, and fault Erangel's spaces for lacking implied meaning and its landmarks for feeling borrowed. — [A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds-new-map-shows-the-imp) [F]; [Heterotopias](https://www.heterotopiaszine.com/2017/12/07/uncanny-island-playerunknowns-battlegrounds/) [F]
- Vondel is called one of the best battle royale maps because it is small and the most densely packed, has varied points of interest that feel distinct, gives each townhouse neighbourhood different roof colours, uses unique wall art (a single tiger mural) for callouts, marks enterable doors with small steps and rooftop routes with planks visible from the air, and balances spacing, cover and sightlines; designers call pacing the most important part. Verdansk and Caldera are contrasted as having large empty stretches. — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/?p=3397705) [F]
- PUBG narrows a large pool of ideas to roughly 10–15 key areas per map, builds the big picture before small details and easter eggs, and rebalances using heat-map data on under-used areas. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/vikendi-making-of-interview) [F]; [App Trigger](https://apptrigger.com/2019/07/19/pubg-update-41-dave-curd-world-art-director-interview) [F]
- Apex: Respawn regularly swaps points of interest on older maps, describes a bigger-picture approach to whole-map layout, added a new point of interest to Kings Canyon in Season 14 to spread landings, and says adjusting maps from fan feedback has been beneficial. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/apex-legends-maps-every-battle-royale-maps-history-and-how-theyve-changed-the-game/1100-6498232/) [S]; [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/apex-legends-season-14-interview-respawn) [S]; [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/apex-legends-season-14-features-a-major-kings-canyon-update/) [S]; [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/adjusting-apex-legends-maps-based-on-fan-feedback-has-been-a-benefit-for-us-says-design-director/1100-6493258/) [S]
- Fortnite formally tiers its places into named locations (often alliterative), landmarks and unnamed spots that the community names itself. — [Fortnite wiki](https://fortnite.fandom.com/wiki/Named_Locations) [S]
- Realm Royale's forges announce themselves with chimney smoke and clinking, creating readable mid-match hotspots. — [PCGamesN](https://pcgamesn.com/realm-royale/realm-royale-pc-review) [F]
- Small maps suit small or low-spec games: Free Fire uses two small, simpler maps, 50 players and roughly 10-minute matches. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/analysing-the-success-of-free-fire-the-other-king-of-battle-royal) [F]
- Maps that copy another game's places are noticed and marked down (Radical Heights, Fear the Wolves). — [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/early-access-review-radical-heights/) [F]; [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/?p=61981) [S]

### Inferences
- A real, recognisable place is an asset no asset pack can supply: the benchmark studios went to considerable trouble to borrow real-world identity, and a real campus provides layout, landmark names and player familiarity for free.
- Players' attachment is to named places and remembered routes, not to texture quality, so effort is best spent making a dozen or so landmarks unmistakable in silhouette and colour and letting generic buildings stay simple and modular.
- Follow the benchmark rule of layout over literal accuracy: keep the real campus recognisable but adjust cover, elevation and spacing for play, as PUBG did.
- Give each zone its own colour or roof treatment and one unique visual marker for callouts, as Vondel does; this also directly counters the repetition that makes procedural buildings look cheap.
- A small, dense map with short matches fits both a small player count and browser performance limits, and avoids the empty stretches criticised in larger maps.
